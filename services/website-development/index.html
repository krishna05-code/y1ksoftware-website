Website FAQs

Questions before starting

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. The first step is to review the current structure, content, technical constraints, and business goals before defining the redesign scope.

Will the website work on mobile?

Responsive behavior is part of the development approach, including readable content, touch-friendly navigation, and adaptable layouts.

Can the project include SEO foundations?

Yes. Technical structure, descriptive metadata, internal linking, useful page content, and performance can be planned into the build. Rankings cannot be guaranteed.

What do you need for a quote?

Share your goals, required pages or features, current website if one exists, and any timing considerations.
