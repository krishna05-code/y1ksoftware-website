Privacy Policy - Money Splitter

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Y1K Software handles information in the Money Splitter Android application (the "App").

The App helps you organize groups and events, record shared expenses, calculate each member's share, and track settlements on your device.

1. Summary

Question Answer
Does the App require an account? No
Does Y1K Software receive your expense or member information? No
Does the App use analytics, ads, or trackers? No
Where is your information stored? In the App's private local storage; copies may also exist in backup files or Android-managed backups
Can you delete your information? Yes, within the App, through Android settings, or by uninstalling the App

2. Information Processed by the App

You may enter event or group titles, member names, expense descriptions, amounts, expense dates, event dates, organizer and contribution details, and settlement information. The App also saves your selected currency and local App settings.

This information is processed on your device to organize events, record shared expenses, calculate shares and settlements, and display event history. The App does not require account creation or sign-in. Y1K Software does not receive your entered expense or member information, and the App has no backend service for that information.

3. Storage and Security

App information is stored locally in the App's private storage using the device's local database and settings storage. Access to that storage is governed by Android and your device's security settings. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or its App data may be able to access the information in the App. We recommend using your device's screen lock and encryption features.

4. Backup and Restore

You may choose to create a backup file. The backup contains your locally stored events, transactions, history, reusable groups, and currency settings. You select the destination through Android's document picker. The file is saved where you choose, and its security and availability are then subject to that destination or service.

You may also choose a backup file to restore. Restoring a backup replaces the App's current data. Keep backup files in a location you trust and delete them when you no longer need them.

5. Android Device Backup

Android backup may be enabled for the App by the operating system, depending on your device, Android version, account settings, and manufacturer configuration. Any operating-system-managed backup or device transfer is controlled by Android and your device provider. You can review Android's backup settings on your device.

6. Permissions and Network Access

The App's expense-management features work offline. It does not request broad storage access; backup and restore use Android's document picker. Opening the Google Play Store or a browser for the optional Rating action requires an available external app and may use that app's network connection.

7. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Money Splitter does not include advertising or analytics services and does not send information you enter in the App to third parties. When you choose a backup destination or restore a file, Android's document picker may show storage providers or apps installed on your device. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

If you select the Rating option, the App can open the Google Play Store or a browser. Those services are governed by their respective providers' privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information remains in the App until you edit or delete it using the available controls. You can also delete the App's locally stored data through Android settings or by uninstalling the App. Deleting local App data does not delete separate backup files; you must remove those from their saved locations. Restoring a backup later can restore the information it contains.

9. Children's Privacy

Money Splitter is a general-purpose expense organizer and is not specifically directed to children. The App does not knowingly collect information from users through a server.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's information practices or functionality change. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Please review the latest policy distributed with the App.
