Expense Tracker

Expense Tracker is a simple personal finance application designed to help you record income and expenses, organize spending, plan monthly budgets, and understand your financial activity.

  • Track income, expenses and savings
  • Organize transactions by category
  • View daily and monthly reports
  • Visualize spending using charts
  • Create monthly category budgets
  • Export expense data to CSV and PDF
  • Backup and restore your local data
