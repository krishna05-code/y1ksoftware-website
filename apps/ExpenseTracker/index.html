Expense Tracker

A simple personal finance app to track income, expenses and budgets.

Overview

Expense Tracker helps you record daily income and expenses, organize transactions by category, and understand your spending habits over time. All data stays on your device.

Features

  • Track income, expenses and savings
  • Organize transactions by category
  • View daily and monthly reports
  • Visualize spending using charts
  • Create monthly category budgets
  • Export expense data to CSV and PDF
  • Backup and restore your local data
