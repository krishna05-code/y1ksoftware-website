Features

A clearer view of everyday money

Expense Tracker helps you record transactions by category and review what happened across a day or month. Data stays on your device.

  • Track income, expenses, and savings
  • Organize transactions by category
  • View daily and monthly reports
  • Visualize spending with charts
  • Create monthly category budgets
  • Export data to CSV and PDF
  • Back up and restore local data
