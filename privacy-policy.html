Privacy Policy - Expense Tracker

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Y1K Software handles information in the Expense Tracker Android application (the "App").

The App helps you track personal expenses, budgets, and reports directly on your device.

1. Summary

Question Answer
Does the App collect personal data? No
Does the App send data to a server or the internet? No
Does the App use analytics, ads, or trackers? No
Where is your data stored? Locally on your device only
Can you delete your data? Yes, anytime, from within the App

2. Data We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. All information you enter — including expenses, categories, budgets/planner entries, notes, amounts, dates, and your selected currency/country preference — is stored exclusively in a local database (Room/SQLite) on your device.

We do not require account creation, sign-in, or any personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.) to use the App.

3. Permissions Used

The App requests the following device permissions, used strictly as described:

  • Storage access (read/write) — used only when you explicitly choose to Backup your data to a file location you select, or Restore your data from a previously saved backup file. These files stay on your device or in a storage location you control (e.g., a folder you pick); we never access, upload, or view these files.

The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or any other sensitive device permission.

4. Backup & Restore

The App includes an optional Backup/Restore feature. When you tap "Backup", you choose a file destination on your device (or connected storage), and a copy of your local database is saved there. "Restore" lets you reload that data back into the App. This process is entirely local — no data ever leaves your device or is sent to us or any server.

5. Clear All Data

You can permanently delete all your data at any time using the Clear All Data option in the navigation drawer. This action is irreversible unless you have a backup file saved.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or cloud services. No data is shared with third parties because none is collected or transmitted in the first place.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, since it does not collect any personal data at all.

8. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device and is never transmitted, your data is only as secure as your device itself. We recommend using your device's built-in security features (screen lock, encryption) to protect your data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
